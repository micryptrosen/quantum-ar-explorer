# QAR-012 discovery refinement

This implementation addendum follows the owner-approved brief and QAR-011
Comparison Chamber. The three-stage beginner lesson remains the primary path.
The optional discovery is intended to make the phase/probability relationship
understandable without prior gate notation. Implementation and test results are
reported separately in the public verification document.

Two independent qubits can have the same immediate Z-basis measurement odds.
H prepares both with equal odds; Z changes A's relative phase while B waits.
A final H on each changes their probabilities to A=1 and B=0. Ordinary
measurement then tests those predictions. The intermediate 50/50 states must
never be described as already having different certain measurement outcomes.

Plain-language presentation is separate from scientific state. Reachable
technical details preserve amplitudes, phase, gate history, probabilities and
the distinction between predictions and sampled measurements. Automated checks
and heuristic review do not establish human novice comprehension. A neutral
observation protocol is provided for an actual participant.

The existing 15 cm comparison instruments, 18 cm primary diagram, 110 cm initial
distance, 6 cm controls and 2.2 cm gaps remain the starting visual constraints.
Placement is deliberate and manual. Preview evidence does not establish physical
SPECS comfort, additive-display contrast, hand tracking or device performance.

Future Veristio adaptations remain proposals. No website, mobile, Authors Resort,
publication companion, commercial platform or broader integration is implemented
by this tranche. Owner review, uploads, attestations and submission remain pending.
