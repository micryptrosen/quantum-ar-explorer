# Product excellence implementation addendum

The owner authorized these additions in QAR-011. The approved planning brief and
the QAR-009 and QAR-010 addenda remain preserved. The beginner experience retains
Meet the Bit, Meet the Qubit and Make a Measurement, followed by its challenge
and completion.

Larger body, control and pole labels, a thicker state vector, contextual Help
and concise opening/completion guidance improve the simulated preview. Scientific
state still commits immediately; brief arrow motion is illustrative, interruptible
and optional. Motion off does not alter operations, sampling or experiment state.
Measurement Next is visible in the display row and follows the existing core
eligibility, including continuation before measuring. Current prediction/repeat
odds are distinguished from ten fresh equal-superposition trials with 50% for
each outcome. Counts always reflect actual observations.

The optional Comparison Chamber is entered from the independent Gate Lab. Two
15 cm diagrams fit side by side inside the 52 cm station, initially 110 cm away.
Controls remain 6 cm high, separated by at least 2.2 cm. Experiment A starts at 0
and applies H-Z-H; B starts at 0 and applies H-H. After A's Z, both states have
50/50 immediate Z-basis odds, but opposite X directions and relative phases pi
and zero. The final H yields computed predictions A=1 and B=0. The Measure step
uses the ordinary validated sampler on those resulting basis states. It does
not select a dramatic random outcome or substitute a recorded result.

The comparison owns independent state, counters, history and revisions. Reset
starts both experiments again. Return restores the exact Gate Lab and beginner
snapshots. A mode-boundary settling guard prevents a release from continuing into
a newly revealed control. The disabled-feature path hides the second renderer
and retains the complete beginner lesson and original lab. The extra renderer's
geometry is allocated at startup even when hidden; this cost remains disclosed.

Two diagrams contain 7,056 authored triangles and 7,056 vertices, versus 3,528
for the original single instrument. Scene-object counts depend on the active
mode and test interactors. Short native Perfetto traces were captured, but no
decoded frame-time, FPS, allocation, latency or battery result is established.
Idle update events settle after animation/feedback. This is preview evidence,
not a wearable performance guarantee.

Actual preview testing uses Lens Studio 5.23.2, SPECS 27, Sunlit Outdoor and Plane.
CLAD/MCP coordinate pinch and LEAF simulated IK are verified separately. Ordinary
Windows mouse activation remains unresolved; no vendor code, collider or
application safeguard was bypassed. Physical glasses, real hand tracking,
additive-display contrast, wearer comfort and novice usability remain untested.
Placement remains deliberate and manual, with Reposition and Recenter; there is
no automatic surface recognition or cross-session physical anchor.

See the repository's current verification and open-and-test documents for final
source/reconstruction test counts, demonstration provenance and evaluation steps.
