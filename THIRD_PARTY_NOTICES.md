Lens Studio **5.23.2.26081320**. Template render resources include `Echopark.hdr`,
the device-camera texture and render target. The unlit graph shader was created
through Lens Studio's supported material tooling. These vendor resources retain
their original ownership and notices; Veristio does not claim ownership of them.
